## 1. The Kill List — Patterns Permanently Outlawed in v6.0

Before evaluating concepts, we explicitly outlaw every "AI-generated SaaS dashboard" anti-pattern that degrades prestige game hardware:

1. **NO Tailwind / SaaS Rounded Cards:** Zero soft-gray boxes with `border-radius: 12px`, drop shadows, and stacked text columns. Every surface is a **machined plate, brushed anodized bezel, smoked optical glass pane, or velvet-lined vitrine** with directional chamfer highlights (`1px` top specular edge, `1px` bottom dark bevel).
2. **NO `backdrop-filter: blur(...)` Over WebGL2:** Blur filters smear live 3D PBR stage lighting and cut mobile GPU headroom in half. Glass plates use sharp smoked-obsidian translucent gradients + metallic hairline specular edges so the 3D stage stays razor-crisp at 60fps.
3. **NO Blotchy or Stroked Text:** Zero `<svg><text>`, zero `<feGaussianBlur>` on type, zero `paint-order: stroke` cartoon outlines. All labels render via native OS subpixel text (`-webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased; text-rendering: geometricPrecision;`) in `'Cinzel'` (display engraving), `'Inter'` (precision UI), and `'JetBrains Mono'` (horological/telemetry readouts with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
4. **NO Generic Browser `<input type="range">` or Checkbox Toggles:** Controls are machined Penny & Giles-style linear fader tracks with recessed slot shadows and knurled brass fader caps, detented Marconi rotary knobs with live pointer angular drag, mechanical rocker/toggle bats, illuminated ballistic amber VU meters, and mil-spec flip-cover safety guards for destructive career wipes.
5. **NO Vertical Scrollbars on Phones:** Every root screen (`GrandLobbyHubV6`, `TrophyMedalVault3DV6`, `BroadcastOptionsConsoleV6`, `PostMatchCeremonyV6`) is hard-locked to `position: fixed; inset: 0; width: 100vw; height: 100dvh; overflow: hidden;`, verified to survive real mobile browser chrome safe heights (`360×560`, `390×660`, `660×340`, `844×390`) even at `112%` text scale.
6. **NO Flat 2D Achievement Icons or OS Emojis:** Every one of the 36 honors is a genuine procedural 3D PBR Three.js object (12 multi-part sculpted championship trophies + 24 struck high-relief/cloisonné medals) inspectable 360° under animated museum spotlights, and every UI glyph is pure geometric vector stroke iconography.

## 3. Synthesized v6.0 Architecture ("Theatrical Broadcast Instrument")

Rather than picking a single sterile direction, v6.0 synthesizes the strongest physical laws from Concepts **1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5**:

### 3.1 `src/styles/ultrahd_v6.css` — The `3x` Super Retina Zero-Scroll Spine
- **Viewport Hard-Lock:** `#app`, `#ui-root.v6-ultrahd-root`, and `.v6-screen` enforce `position: fixed; inset: 0; width: 100vw; height: 100dvh; overflow: hidden;` with `env(safe-area-inset-*)` padding tokens.
- **Stage-World Dynamic Skinning:** Every surface inherits `--v6-stage-surface`, `--v6-stage-border`, `--v6-stage-accent`, `--v6-stage-badge-bg`, `--v6-stage-badge-text`, and `--v6-font-heading` injected live from `StageAtmosphereThemeV6.hudTheme` so switching between Stage 01 (Vaudeville Mahogany/Brass), Stage 05 (1969 Bunker Olive/Nixie), Stage 06 (Kyoto Urushi Vermilion/Kintsugi), or Stage 10 (Colosseum Nero Marble/24k Gold) physically re-skins the brass-and-glass hardware in real time.
- **Live Accessibility & Ergonomics Tokens:**
  - `data-v6-text-scale="0.95" | "1" | "1.12"` dynamically scales `--v6-text-scale` while responsive `clamp()` ceilings prevent overflow even at `1.12` on `360×560`.
  - `data-v6-thumb="CENTER" | "LEFT_HAND" | "RIGHT_HAND"` ergonomically biases bottom primary action decks, carousel steppers, and fader bays toward the player's active thumb.
  - `data-v6-vision="STANDARD" | "DEUTERANOPIA" | "PROTANOPIA" | "TRITANOPIA"` applies perceptually verified semantic accent overrides (Okabe-Ito / Tol high-contrast stage signal hues) without distorting 3D metallic neutrals.
