## 1. 1960s TV GAME SHOW ENCYCLOPEDIC DESIGN LAWS ("IN LIVING COLOR", 1963–1969)

1. **Broadcast Camera & Color Physics (RCA TK-41 / Norelco PC-70 Look):**
   - 1960s color shows flooded sets with `300+` footcandles and celebrated saturated Mid-Century hues against **deep Peacock Navy (`#0D2238`), Warm Walnut (`#5C301B`), or Charcoal Slate (`#16202A`)** studio surrounds so translucent backlit **Acrylite/Plexiglas** lightboxes and spinning **Trilon** tiles popped off the screen.
   - Avoid dead `#FFFFFF` backgrounds (causes NTSC halation); use **Warm Broadcast Cream (`#F7EEDB` / `#FFF6E3`)** with crisp dark ink (`#141B29`) for supreme mobile legibility (`>= 7:1` contrast).
2. **Authentic 1960s Broadcast Typography (Ban Coding Fonts in Player UI):**
   - **Show Logos & Big Splash Headers:** Chunky Mid-Century display faces (*Cooper Black* -> `Caprasimo` / `Fraunces` Black; *Broadway/Lubalin* kinetic caps).
   - **Tote Boards, Dollar Values, Category Headers & Clocks:** Squared-off 1962 *Eurostile / Microgramma* (`Michroma` / `Chakra Petch`) and geometric *Futura / Avant Garde* (`Jost` / `League Spartan` ExtraBold, `ALL CAPS`, `+0.04em..0.08em` tracking).
   - **Question Clues & 4-Option Answers:** Clean, high-x-height humanist/geometric sans or sturdy slab (`Plus Jakarta Sans` SemiBold/Bold or `Zilla Slab`) at **`18px–22px` on mobile** and **`22px–28px` on tablet/desktop**. Never use developer monospace (`JetBrains Mono`) for main game chrome!
3. **Physical Scenic Grammar:**
   - **Motorized 3-Sided Trilons** (*Concentration*): Tiles physically rotate `120°/180°` on brass/aluminum spindles with a crisp mechanical clack (`Category/Dollar Face -> Twist/Reveal Face -> Claimed Color Badge`).
   - **Chasing `#11` Incandescent Marquee Bulbs:** Warm `2700K` tungsten bulbs (`#FFD166` core + soft amber halo) chasing sequentially around title frames, Secret Squares, and winning rows.
   - **1960s Cold-Open Formula (Under 30 Seconds, Skippable Anytime):**
     1. *Teaser Hook (5s):* Who you are & the stakes (*"Live from Studio 6A in Living Color—50 million Americans are watching..."*).
     2. *Core Loop (15s):* Tap a tile on the Tote Board -> answer before the studio clock expires -> stack dollars & trigger board twists.
     3. *Win Condition (10s):* Outbank the reigning Champion across 10 turns to claim the Purse and advance the backstage mystery!
